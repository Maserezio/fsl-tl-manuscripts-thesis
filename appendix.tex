\section{Training Settings}
\label{app:training-settings}

\label{sec:training-setup}\label{sec:training-semantic}\label{sec:training-two-stage}\label{sec:training-mask-rcnn}\label{sec:training-center-line}\label{sec:training-pretraining}
Tables~\ref{tab:training-settings}--\ref{tab:center-line-params} list the training settings. Every line polygon and every connected component of a U-DIADS-TL mask becomes one instance mask and one \gls{bbox}. The checkpoint, the thresholds, and the postprocessing parameters are selected on the validation pages, except for the center-line model. The BBox U-Net is trained on \gls{gt} crops of the same training pages as the detectors. In \gls{rq}~1, it uses the Tversky loss on U-DIADS-TL and \gls{bce} on DIVA-HisDB. On Mask R-CNN boxes, the confidence threshold is selected by the box F1 score at an \gls{iou} of 0.5, and the padding, the mask threshold, and the minimum instance area by \gls{fm}. The transfer configuration of \gls{rq}~3 was selected on the validation pages of nine source--target pairs.
