\chapter{RQ3 Configuration Selection}
\label{app:rq3-extra}

The configuration of Section~\ref{sec:cross-generalization} was selected starting from a baseline that differs in two respects: its proposal network is reinitialized with line-oriented anchors of 16 to 256 pixels and height-to-width ratios of 0.05, 0.1, and 0.2, and it uses no scale jitter.

\FloatBarrier
\section{Configuration Ablation}
\label{sec:rq3-robust}\label{sec:results-rq3-robust}\label{sec:results-rq3-failures}

Five modifications of the baseline were switched on cumulatively:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] The proposal network of the CATMuS pretraining is retained.
    \item[(b)] A train-only scale jitter is applied before the canvas fit, with an enlargement bound \(s_{\max}\) of 1.6 or 2.0.
    \item[(c)] The ROI size of the mask branch is increased from 14 to 28.
    \item[(d)] Box non-maximum suppression thresholds of 0.5 and 0.7 are added to the validation grid.
    \item[(e)] The ConvNeXt stem and its first two stages are frozen, and the checkpoint is selected by early stopping on the source-validation \gls{fm}.
\end{enumerate}
An optional post-processing step merges line fragments and removes very small, very tall, or off-text masks. The modifications are compared on nine source--target pairs, and a configuration is chosen by the mean source-validation \gls{fm} of its five source models, so that no target test page influences the choice.

The scale jitter~(b) is the decisive modification (Table~\ref{tab:rq3-ablation}): together with~(a), it raises ONB$\to$GRPOLY from 7.84\% to 73.59\% and RASAM$\to$GRPOLY from 8.01\% to 90.36\% \gls{fm}. Modifications (c)--(e), \(s_{\max}=2.0\), and post-processing do not raise the mean source-validation \gls{fm} of 96.35\%, so configuration \emph{ab} with \(s_{\max}=1.6\) is selected. With the same NorHand v3 training pages, it raises the mean in-domain \gls{fm} of protocol~A from 84.96\% to 88.61\% and the mean transfer from 56.44\% to 70.56\%.

The first NorHand v3 training triple, an envelope, a card, and a letter annotated with narrow baseline strips, did not represent the test pages and yielded 33.17\% in-domain \gls{fm}; three other random triples from the same pool yielded 81.15--82.63\%. The final configuration therefore uses a second draw.
