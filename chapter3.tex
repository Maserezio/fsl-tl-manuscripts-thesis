\chapter{Research Questions}
\label{ch:rqs}
Based on the challenges in the field of text line segmentation and after conducting a literature review in Chapter \ref{lit_rec} and considering its conclusions, several Research Question (RQs) were formulated:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item How well, in terms of the line detection metrics, does an FSL model using a transformer-based backbone perform compared to a CNN backbone for text line segmentation in historical manuscripts?
    \item How many labeled images are needed to achieve performance comparable to SOTA methods, as measured by the line detection metrics?
    \item How do the heterogeneity and quality (e.g., Latin-only vs. mixed scripts; clean vs. mixed clean and degraded vs. only degraded) of training documents affect the performance of text line segmentation model, i.e., does manuscript diversity impact FSL model robustness?
\end{enumerate}

The line detection metrics mentioned in RQ1 and used for evaluation are described in Chapter \ref{metrics}
